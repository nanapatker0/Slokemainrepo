Hi, I’m Sloke.

I’m a filmmaker and photographer drawn to small, human stories. Most of my work starts with a person, a place and enough time for something real to happen in front of the camera.

I shoot short films, documentaries and portrait series, and take commissions from brands, musicians and families. I handle the work from first idea to final grade, so the look stays consistent from the first frame to the last.
